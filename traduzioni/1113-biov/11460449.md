# Bipolaris_glycines

*[Voce originale su Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bipolaris_glycines)*

***Bipolaris glycines***, or **Drechslera blight**, is a fungal plant pathogen of soybeans.

It was originally published as *Drechslera glycini* S. Naray. & Durairaj in Madras agric. J. 58 (8): 712 (1971) before being resolved in 1992 as a synonym of *Bipolaris glycines*.

It was found on the leaves of *Glycine max* (soyabean) in Tamil Nadu, India.
