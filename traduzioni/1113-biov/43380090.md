# Biqraqa

*[Voce originale su Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biqraqa)*

**Biqraqa** (Arabic: بقراقة) is a Syrian village located in the Masyaf Subdistrict in Masyaf District, located west of Hama. According to the Syria Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS), Biqraqa had a population of 798 in the 2004 census.
