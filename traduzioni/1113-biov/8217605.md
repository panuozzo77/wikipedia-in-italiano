# Biphenol

*[Voce originale su Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biphenol)*

In organic chemistry, a **biphenol** refers to compounds with the formula (C6H4OH)2. Such compounds formally result from the coupling of two phenols. All are colorless solids with the formula (C6H4OH)2.
Three symmetrical isomers of biphenol exist:
