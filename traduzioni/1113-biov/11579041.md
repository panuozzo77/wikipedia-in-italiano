# Bir_al-Maksur

*[Voce originale su Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bir_al-Maksur)*

**Bir al-Maksur** or **Beer el-Maksura** (Arabic: بئر المكسور; Hebrew: בִּיר אל-מַכְּסוּר) is an Arab Bedouin local council in the Northern District of Israel located 15 kilometres (9.3 mi) north-west of Nazareth. In 2024 its population was 11,068. The villagers belong to the Arab el-Hujeirat Bedouin tribe, settled there in the 1950s. In 2022, 100% of the population was Muslim.

## History

### Before Common Era

Flint from the Mousterian culture, made with the Levallois technique, in addition to remains from Pre-Pottery Neolithic A and B have been found during excavations.

Sherds from Iron age I, and possibly Iron age II have also been found.

### Common Era

A burial cave, with ceramics and artefacts dating to the late Roman period, that is, 3rd–4th centuries CE, has been unearthed.

In 1881, the PEF's *Survey of Western Palestine* noted at *Kh. el Maksur*: "Heaps of stones."
