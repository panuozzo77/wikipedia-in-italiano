# Bir_Küçük_Aşk

*[Voce originale su Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bir_K%C3%BC%C3%A7%C3%BCk_A%C5%9Fk)*

***Bir Küçük Aşk*** (English: A Small Love) is İzel's third studio album. All of the lyrics were written by Altan Çetin. "Yok Yere" made a huge success all over Turkey.
