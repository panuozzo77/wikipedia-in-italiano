# Biqasqas

*[Voce originale su Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biqasqas)*

**Biqasqas** (Arabic: بقصقص, also known as **Qusqus**) is a Syrian village located in the Masyaf Subdistrict in Masyaf District, located west of Hama. According to the Syria Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS), Biqasqas had a population of 514 in the 2004 census.
