# Bir_Protik

*[Voce originale su Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bir_Protik)*

**Bir Protik** (Bengali: বীর প্রতীক, lit. 'Symbol of Bravery or Idol of Courage') is the fourth highest gallantry award in Bangladesh.

## Recipients

This award was declared on 15 December 1973. A total of 426 people have received the award so far, all for their actions during the liberation war of Bangladesh in 1971.
